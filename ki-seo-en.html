The new search

Is your name in the answer?

Ask ChatGPT: "Who is the best accountant in my town?" You get two or three names. Not a list of ten links, but a recommendation. If you are not on it, nobody even contacts you.

The AI does not make up its answer. It builds it from what it can find about you online:

  • your website and how clearly it explains what you do
  • your reviews on Google and other platforms
  • listings in business directories
  • mentions on other sites, in articles and guides

If that is thin or contradictory, the AI would rather name your competitors. And no, you do not need a self-proclaimed "AI guru" for this. It is solid work in the right places.

Frequently asked questions

Is AI SEO different from regular SEO?

There is a lot of overlap. If you do well on Google, you also have better odds with ChatGPT. But the AI looks beyond your website: at reviews, directories and who else writes about you. That is where we add extra work.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend me?

No. Nobody can control what an AI answers, and anyone who promises that is not telling the truth. We improve the material the AI builds its answer from. For Liftpictures, that is exactly what worked.
