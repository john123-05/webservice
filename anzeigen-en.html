The principle behind it

How to win new customers on autopilot

Think of it like a funnel:

You reach 1,000 people with one ad.

Of course, not all of them become customers right away. But roughly 1 to 3% click and land on your website, so about 30 people.

Of those 30, maybe 3 book a call because they find your offer interesting.

And in the end, that realistically turns into 1 new customer.

You reinvest part of it in advertising, so even more people see you and get in touch.

You win customers and generate revenue.

And then this process repeats again and again.

The biggest advantage: this process is predictable and scalable.

When it gets expensive

5 reasons why most campaigns only cost money and bring no customers

Your ad is so boring that people keep scrolling: you are not speaking to your target audience the right way.

No story

Wrong technical setup

Google and Meta show your ad to the wrong people because the setup was wrong from the start.

No conversion tracking

Without clean tracking you don't know which ad brings inquiries, and you keep paying blindly.

No optimization

Your budget goes up in smoke because nobody continuously analyzes what works and what doesn't.
