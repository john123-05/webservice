What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

With Google Ads you buy the top spot on Google, which means ongoing costs and paying per click. SEO takes a little longer (about 90 days), but after that it is permanently free and usually delivers much better results. The best results come from combining both. However, your SEO foundation should be in place before you start with Google Ads or similar.

Which businesses do you work with?

We mainly work with local service providers and trades businesses: companies that want to be found on Google Maps and need real inquiries, not just clicks.
