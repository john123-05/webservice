Case study · Liftpictures

How Liftpictures generates more inquiries internationally

Liftpictures provides photo and video systems for theme parks and attractions worldwide. The original website was aimed at the German market and had no clear path to lead generation.

1 · New website for international visitors

The website was restructured so international audiences understand the offer faster and can get in touch more easily.

2 · Lead funnel for lead generation

A short quiz funnel was added. Visitors answer a few questions and receive a matching PDF by email, even without a direct inquiry.

Result

  • Better international visibility
  • Additional lead channel through the quiz funnel
  • A structured path from visitor → inquiry
