Frequently asked questions about business websites

Many businesses ask similar questions when it comes to a new website or a redesign. Here you will find answers to some of the most common questions about planning, execution and results.

How long does it take to build a website?

Building a new website usually takes between 2 and 6 weeks, depending on the scope of the project and how quickly the content is available. Smaller websites can be done faster, while larger projects with several pages or special features need a little more time.

Do I have to provide the copy and images myself?

Not necessarily. A lot of the content can be developed or improved together. If you already have copy, images or logos, we can of course use them. If content is missing, we help you create clear and easy-to-understand content for your website.

Will my website also work well on smartphones?

Yes. Most visitors now reach websites on their smartphones. That is why every website is built to work just as well on mobile devices as on desktop.

Can my website also be found more easily on Google?

A clear page structure, fast loading times and understandable content help search engines understand websites better. That is why we also make sure the technical and content foundations for better visibility are in place.

Can I edit my website myself later?

Yes. Most websites are built with a system that lets you update or extend the content yourself later on. So you stay flexible and can make changes at any time.

Which businesses benefit from a professional website?

A professional website matters for almost every business, especially for local service providers. Many customers research online first before they get in touch or make a decision.
