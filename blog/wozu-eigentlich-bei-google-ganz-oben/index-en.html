Why show up at the top of Google at all?

Published on May 19, 2026 · John

Hi,

showing up at the very top of Google sounds nice at first.

But is all the effort even worth it?

Most businesses already have enough on their plate. One more construction site that nobody really needs.

And sure, I could tell you now that Google is the most important thing of all and should be your top priority right away.

But that would be about as objective as a car dealer telling you that today is exactly the perfect day to buy a car.

So here are three things you can check quite easily to see whether it should be a priority:

Most clicks go to the first three results on Google. Most people don't even scroll further down. If you are not at the top, you usually only get what's left.

And that is exactly why the businesses up there also get the most inquiries, calls and new customers. More visibility automatically means people notice you sooner and build trust.

So the real question is:

If your business ranked higher on Google and you got more inquiries than now, could you turn them into more customers?

If the answer is yes, local visibility is probably worth a lot more than many people think.

If the answer is no, then honestly, you don't need to spend any more time on it.

In the end, it really comes down to one thing:

Do you even want more customer inquiries?

If yes, Google should be pretty high up on your marketing list.
