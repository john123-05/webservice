A simple way to rank higher on Google

Published on July 22, 2026 · John

Hello,

I have a super simple method for you to rank better on Google. And it is easy to put into practice. You don't even need any technical knowledge.

If you run a business, you get reviews. Hopefully they are good. Hopefully you get them fairly regularly.

If not, you should change that. A steady stream of reviews helps your ranking. We'll talk about that another time.

Anyway: most businesses get reviews and then do one of two things:

  1. They are happy about the review but don't reply to it

  2. They are happy about the review and reply politely, something like: “Thank you so much for the kind review, we really appreciate it!”

Option 2 is better than option 1. You should reply to reviews, that is a good idea.

But:

Google is a big robot. It constantly crawls your profile to figure out WHAT you do and WHERE you do it.

Anything you can do to help Google better understand WHAT you do and WHERE you do it is a good thing.

So instead of just replying politely, make sure your reply contains keywords you want to rank for.

For example, if you are a tree surgeon in Springfield, want more jobs in Springfield and get a review from someone in the area, you could reply like this instead:

“Thank you so much for the kind review. It was a pleasure removing your trees in Springfield. We are taking on more and more jobs in Springfield. We would be happy to help you with all your tree care needs in the future too, whether it's tree felling, crown reduction, pruning or anything else!”

That gives the Google bot a full five-course meal instead of just throwing it a few crumbs.

This really helps your SEO results. Give it a try!
