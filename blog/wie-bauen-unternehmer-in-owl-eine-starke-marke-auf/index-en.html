“You need something with social media”, as the boss you have to join the next TikTok trend, or you need a promo video that is cool and funny.

But what for? Well, obviously: branding.

After all, Coca-Cola and big supermarket chains do nothing else.

People have to know your name, see your logo and like your slogans on Instagram.

But here is the reality.

As a mid-sized business, you don't have the budget of Coca-Cola or a supermarket chain.

Sounds simple? It is.

So if you start making funny videos or coming up with cool slogans,

that won't lead your people to buy.
