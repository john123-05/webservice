3. Google Analytics

Use of Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC ("Google"). Google Analytics uses so-called "cookies", text files that are stored on your computer and that allow an analysis of how you use the website.

The information generated by the cookie about your use of this website is usually transferred to a Google server in the USA and stored there. If IP anonymization is activated on this website, however, Google will first shorten your IP address within member states of the European Union or in other states party to the Agreement on the European Economic Area.

Purpose of processing: on behalf of the operator of this website, Google will use this information to evaluate your use of the website, to compile reports on website activity and to provide the website operator with further services related to website and internet use.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest). Our legitimate interest lies in the analysis, optimization and economic operation of our website.

Objection and removal: you can prevent cookies from being stored by adjusting your browser software accordingly. You can also prevent Google from collecting the data generated by the cookie and relating to your use of the website (including your IP address) and from processing this data by downloading and installing the browser plug-in available at the following link: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout?hl=en

Further information: you can find more information on how Google Analytics handles user data in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en

Data processing agreement: we have concluded a data processing agreement with Google and fully implement the strict requirements of the German data protection authorities when using Google Analytics.

4. Meta Pixel (Facebook Pixel)

On individual pages of this website (deadline calendar/newsletter sign-up for fairground operators) we use the Meta Pixel, a service of Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, 4 Grand Canal Square, Grand Canal Harbour, Dublin 2, Ireland ("Meta"). For users outside the EEA, processing may also be carried out by Meta Platforms, Inc.

The Meta Pixel is only loaded after you give your consent via our cookie banner and is only included on the campaign pages mentioned, not across the whole website.

Data collected:

  • IP address and device/browser information
  • the page visited and the time of the visit
  • Conversion events, e.g. that a newsletter sign-up was completed ("Lead")

No names or email addresses are transmitted to Meta via the Meta Pixel. The transfer is technically anonymized via the event data mentioned.

Purpose of processing: Meta uses this data to show us how visitors interact with our ads, to optimize the delivery of our ads and to build suitable target audiences for future campaigns.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent). The Meta Pixel only becomes active after you have selected "Accept all" in the cookie banner. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future via the cookie settings.

Data transfer to the USA: using the Meta Pixel may involve a transfer of data to the USA. Meta is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or uses EU standard contractual clauses as an additional safeguard.

You can find more information on how Meta handles your data in Meta's privacy policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

5. Data collection on this website

Booking and inquiry form

When you request a free intro call or book an appointment, the data you enter is collected and processed.

Data collected:

  • Name (required)
  • Company name (optional)
  • Website (optional)
  • Multiple-choice questions about project goals and desired features
  • preferred start date
  • Email address (required)
  • selected meeting platform (Zoom or Microsoft Teams)
  • selected appointment

Purpose of processing:

The data is used exclusively to schedule the appointment, understand your request and contact you for the agreed call.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent) and Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures).

Storage period: your information is deleted as soon as it is no longer needed for the purpose, but no later than 6 months after the last contact if no business relationship arises.

Use of external services

Appointment booking and data transfer take place via our secure form system. If you choose a meeting platform (Zoom or Microsoft Teams), their privacy policies also apply:

  • Zoom privacy policy
  • Microsoft Teams privacy policy

Automated processing

The integration service Make (Make s.r.o.) is used to transmit your information securely. The data is processed exclusively for transmitting and managing the appointment or sign-up.

Your data is not passed on to any other third parties.

Newsletter and deadline calendar sign-up

On several pages of this website (including the deadline calendar for fairground operators) you can sign up with your email address for reminders about upcoming application deadlines.

Data collected:

  • Email address (required)
  • Phone/WhatsApp number (optional, if you provide it)

Purpose of processing:

We use your email address to remind you in good time before application deadlines expire. These emails occasionally also contain information about our own services for fairground operators (e.g. websites or digital application folders). They are therefore not purely reminder emails, but also advertising within the meaning of the German Unfair Competition Act (UWG).

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, e.g. via the unsubscribe link at the end of every email or by sending a message to kontakt@impress-rank.de.

Storage period: your data is stored until you unsubscribe or withdraw your consent.

Processing and disclosure: your sign-up data is transmitted via the integration service Make to the email tool we use to send reminders. Your data is not passed on to any other third parties.
